# Survival Paradise — Desert Biome Production Checklist

**Status:** In production. 26 Desert/Nature/Tree and Nature/Shrub PNGs approved for prototype use; final in-engine scale verification pending. The remaining 92 assets are not yet approved.

**Scope:** Iraqi and Arabian-influenced arid landscape: sandy plains, gravel flats, stony desert, dry wadis, salt pans, occasional oases, scattered camps and small settlements.

**Production rules:** One asset per isolated transparent PNG; consistent handcrafted gritty survival art; matching oblique/top-down game view, shadows and material scale; no scenery, text, contact sheets, or unrelated objects in output; maintain clear silhouette.

**Naming:** `SP_Desert_[Category]_[Subtype]_[Variant].png`, e.g. `SP_Desert_Shrub_Haloxylon_01.png`. Keep each PNG under `Desert/<Nature|Structures>/<Category>/`.

**Biome rules:** Trees sparse in open desert; date palms concentrated at oases/settlements; ephemeral flowers only after rain; preserve native regional feel, no wild saguaro cactus; one shadow treatment for entire pack.

## QA release conditions

Every sprite must pass: isolated single object; actual alpha channel and transparent perimeter; no labels/scenery; object not cropped; no accidental neighboring fragments; consistent camera, light, texture and scale; clearly distinct from earlier variants; plausible habitat and species; named correctly; in-engine scaling verified.

**Baseline:** Approved Forest Biome Nature Pack (84 files) is separate and unchanged.

**Proposed build sequence:** Nature Trees → Shrubs → Grass → Rocks → Ground → Wood → Structures Shelter → Water → Camp → Building → Roadside → Defense.
