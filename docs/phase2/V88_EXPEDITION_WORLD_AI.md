# V88 expedition/world update

Square bag inventory and bandit loot slots in responsive 6- and 4-column grids; original V84 item artwork retained.

Ten visitable, uniquely rendered, solid landmark buildings: Ranger Cabin, Field Clinic, Rusted Garage, Signal Relay, Supply Depot, Lookout Tower, Abandoned Farm, Road Checkpoint, Desert Motel, Field Workshop. Persistent supply bags at exterior entrances, one-time seeding on existing save migration and New Game, never respawn after looting. HUD nearest-site compass. Uses existing V87 dual-inventory LOOT/LOOT ALL actions.

Bandit tactical finite-state logic: local patrols, pursuit, lateral strafing, cover awareness, retreat at low HP, line-of-sight required for attacks, obstacle sliding, world-distance sleep and animation frame-rate independence.

Two new placeable equipment items: Water Collector (3 wood, 2 stone, 3 fiber, 1 scrap) generates +2 water per 360 world minutes with saved cooldown; Field Shelter (5 wood, 4 fiber, 1 scrap) restores stamina and reduces fatigue, 180-minute cooldown. Portable crafting, aim+USE placement and V84 equipment persistence retained. V84 2-value stock saves migrate into 4-value format.

Two new consumables: bandage (2 fiber + 1 scrap) heals 24 HP; energy ration (1 food + 1 water) restores 50 stamina and reduces fatigue by 20. Stacks persist, new icons and quickbar entries.

All V31 pixel character sprites untouched. Existing world, controls, projectile cover, camera/zoom, inventory, save import/export, and optional permanent APK signing support preserved. Device visuals/performance require user acceptance.
